■ Suggested Career Entry Points
🎯
Start Here — Blue Team
SOC Analyst is the most common entry point. Learn log analysis, alert triage, and SIEM platforms first before branching into specializations.
⚔
Offensive Path — Red Team
Start with Web App Pentesting or Network Pentesting. Earn CEH, eJPT, or OSCP certifications. Bug bounty is a great practice ground.
⚡
Advanced Path — Purple Team
Requires experience in both Red and Blue. Focus on Detection Engineering and MITRE ATT&CK framework mastery after 2–3 years in either team.
📈
Management Path — GRC
Ideal for those with business + security interest. CISM, CISA, ISO 27001 Lead Auditor are key certs. Great salary and work-life balance.
